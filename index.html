Who is FleetNest built for?

FleetNest is designed for taxi operators, car rental businesses, travel agencies and corporate fleet teams that need one system for bookings, duties, fleet, billing and reporting.

Can we manage invoices, receipts and supplier purchases?

Yes. FleetNest includes Billing Management, Receipts, Purchase Invoices, Purchase Payments, and credit or debit notes.

Can our dispatch team use it on mobile?

FleetNest is designed to be responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile, so managers and dispatch teams can stay connected wherever they work.

Which plans include Customer Ledger?

Customer Ledger is included with Popular, Pro and Enterprise plans. Free and Basic provide more limited reporting access.
